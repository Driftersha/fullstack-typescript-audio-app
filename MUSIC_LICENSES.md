## Additional attribution/provenance evidence

- HoliznaCC0 directly states that their catalog is dedicated to CC0 on https://holiznacc0.bandcamp.com/ . Some other works by this artist use public-domain samples, so independently confirming third-party sample rights for each selected track is still useful.
- JMHBM / Beat Mekanik credits composer/producer Johnathon M. Horner on the track pages for Dreams and Electron. No independent sample/license audit has been completed.
- Play House requests optional credit on the FMA track page, despite identifying the track as CC0.
- CC0 deed: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . No warranties are provided and third-party rights may remain.

## Release gate (required before moving any MP3 to public/ or deploying)

- [ ] Download each MP3 from its **exact FMA track page**. Do not use guessed file URLs or unrelated mirrors.
- [ ] Validate playable audio file, track identity and duration; record file SHA-256 and size.
- [ ] Capture dated evidence of CC0 claim for each track and preserve metadata in a private evidence folder.
- [ ] Assess any samples/third-party recordings; request rights clarification from artists where provenance is uncertain.
- [ ] Use original artwork (do not automatically reuse FMA album images).
- [ ] Add credits/license info to website and document removal process.
- [ ] Check browser behavior, performance, security and build; obtain release approval before production deployment.

**No rights guarantee:** CC0 is not a warranty. The legal basis for specific recordings remains subject to true ownership and third-party rights.
